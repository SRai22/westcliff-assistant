## The Big Picture

The system is already built (React + Express + FastAPI + MongoDB). The capstone deliverable is an 8-week cumulative paper + final presentation that frames this as a real university deployment proposal — business case → research → design → implementation plan → maintenance → compiled paper → live presentation.

## Professor's Recurring Themes

1. **"Four Columns"** — every decision filtered through: Business Value, Risk Management, Ethics/Legal, Feasibility
2. **Governance is explicit** — auth, RBAC, FERPA, audit trail must be visible in design
3. **Executive tone** — no "I think", data-driven language, formal business framing
4. **SDLC alignment** — every phase maps to a recognized SDLC stage
5. **Residual risk** — always acknowledge what the project does NOT solve
6. **Screenshots as mock-ups** — the working system is the strongest evidence
