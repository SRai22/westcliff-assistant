## What to Produce

PowerPoint (or equivalent) presentation, 8–10 minutes, delivered live.

### Requirements
- Format as presenting to business leadership for decision and approval
- Must cover all 5 chapters of the project
- No specific slide count — quality over quantity
- Delivered live during final class session

### Suggested Slide Structure

1. **Title slide** — project name, author, Westcliff University, date
2. **The Problem** (1–2 slides) — fragmented student services, no unified intake, 10 departments
3. **The Solution** (1–2 slides) — AI-powered portal, high-level architecture diagram
4. **Research Evidence** (1–2 slides) — key findings from the 4 sources, decision matrix summary
5. **Live Demo** (2–3 minutes) — show the actual working system
   - Student: login → describe issue → AI triage → ticket created
   - Staff: Kanban → ticket detail → AI assist (summarize/draft reply)
6. **Design Highlights** (1–2 slides) — security/governance, FERPA, RBAC, audit trail
7. **Budget & ROI** (1 slide) — 3-year TCO comparison table
8. **Implementation & Maintenance** (1 slide) — sprint summary, ongoing maintenance approach
9. **Conclusion & Recommendation** (1 slide) — ask for approval

### Key Advantage
The system is live and working — demo it. This is stronger than any slide deck.

### Prep Tasks
- [ ] Build slide deck
- [ ] Ensure system runs cleanly for demo (`docker compose up --build`)
- [ ] Seed demo data that tells a clear story
- [ ] Practice to fit 8–10 minute window
- [ ] Prepare for Q&A on budget, risk, and governance
