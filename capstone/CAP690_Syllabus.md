**Westcliff University | College of Technology & Engineering**

## Course Description

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Course Number & Name** | CAP 690 Masters Applied Capstone |
| **Units** | 3 |
| **Length of Class** | 8 Weeks |
| **Required Text** | None. Materials and resources are provided in the course as appropriate. |
| **Instructional Method** | Online / On-Campus |

**Description:** This course allows COTE graduate students to research and present solutions to industry problems through the planned development, construction, implementation, and management of an information technology project. Learning outcomes from all previous program courses are presented in a pragmatic and value-added manner with the course culminating in a final written project-based plan and presentation. The purpose of the project is to specifically address a business-related technology issue, provide business justification, research and evaluate potential solutions, make a recommendation based on determined decision criteria, and design and plan the implementation of the new solution. The instructor serves as a mentor and consultant in the creation, development, implementation, and management of the project plan. The solution must demonstrate mastery of program and course learning outcomes, and is presented in a final PowerPoint presentation.

## Detailed Course Outline

### Week 1

#### Week 1 Discussion
Discuss the following using program knowledge and additional research:
1. The factors involved in the evaluation of an Information Technology project
2. The factors that must be considered when implementing short- versus long-term solutions
3. The value these solutions can bring to the organization

#### Project Plan Determination
During the first live session, discuss and determine the capstone project and develop a plan for completion with the instructor and classmates.

---

### Week 2

#### Professional Assignment 1 – Phase 1: Background, Business Justification, and Project Introduction
- Provide information about the selected organization, including current status and existing issues to be addressed.
- Serves as the foundation for business justification in seeking executive approval.
- Present a recommendation for technical work to address the identified problem(s).
- Written in formal tone, addressed to business leadership for their approval.
- **Length:** At least 5–7 pages
- **Citations:** At least 3

---

### Week 3

#### Professional Assignment 2 – Project Research and Annotated Bibliography
- Research a minimum of 3–4 different solution options relevant to the project.
- Create an annotated bibliography with a brief summary of each option.
- Will be used when writing Phase 2 of the capstone project.
- All references in proper APA 7 format.
- Include a cover page; a separate references page is not required (embed each reference within the paper).

---

### Week 4

#### Week 4 SM Assignment
Student Socratic Metric (SM) Assignment — two parts: Socratic Metric Activity and Socratic Metric Discussion Question.

**Socratic Metric Activity instructions:**
- Open the SM assignment in your GAP course.
- Read each question aloud (≥70% captured by the system), then answer in natural speaking voice.
- Do not read from a script or AI-generated text.
- Read the system's feedback aloud before refining; may revise up to 4 times.
- Select "Finish Assessment" and wait for the Assignment Complete screen before leaving.

#### Professional Assignment 3 – Phase 2: Research and Recommendation
- Summarize research from the annotated bibliography and any additional sources.
- Provide a recommendation and describe the decision criteria used.
- Written in formal tone for business leadership approval.
- Provide enough detail for both technical and business audiences; avoid excessive technical specifications or code.
- **Length:** At least 5–7 pages
- May be appended to Phase 1 to build the cumulative project paper.

---

### Week 5

#### Professional Assignment 4 – Phase 3: Project Design
- Provide full details for the proposed project solution (the "meat" of the project).
- Include all information needed to make the solution work:
  - Mock-ups, brief code descriptions, or functioning code (if software/web)
  - Network diagrams (if network solution)
  - Security equipment/tools/methods (if security solution)
- Include full written functional descriptions of the solution.
- Include required budget information for business planning.
- **Length:** 7–10 pages
- May be appended to the ongoing project paper.

---

### Week 6

#### Professional Assignment 5 – Phase 4: Implementation
- Lay out the full implementation plan including project schedule, individual tasks, and timelines.
- Examples: hardware acquisition, wiring, programming, training, agile sprint breakdowns, code/framework examples.
- Include any required training for use or administration.
- **Length:** 4–5 pages
- May be appended to the ongoing project paper.

#### Professional Assignment 5 – Phase 5: Ongoing Maintenance and Recommendations
- Discuss ongoing maintenance and support of the implemented solution.
- May include: technical staff training, disaster recovery plans, facilities management, software updates.
- Include any future work recommendations following project completion.
- **Length:** 2–3 pages
- May be appended to the ongoing project paper.

---

### Week 7

#### Comprehensive Learning Assessment (CLA) – Full Project Paper
Compile all 5 phases into a single cohesive final project plan.

**Required components:**
- Cover page
- Executive summary
- Table of contents (including list of tables and list of images, if used)
- Individual chapter for each of the 5 phases
- Final conclusion (following Chapter 5)
- Final references section (APA 7)

**Expected length:** ~26–38 pages or longer (no strict minimum, based on per-chapter page guidance).

---

### Week 8

#### Final Project Presentation
- Develop and present a PowerPoint (or equivalent) presentation.
- Format as though presenting to business leadership for their decision and approval.
- Must address all 5 chapters of the project.
- **Duration:** Approximately 8–10 minutes (depending on class size)
- Delivered live during the final class session.
- No specific slide count required.

#### Week 8 SM Assignment
Same format as Week 4 SM Assignment — Socratic Metric Activity and Discussion Question. Refer to SM Student Guide for full instructions.
