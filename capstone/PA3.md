## 🏛️ Phase 2: Structural Blueprint

### 1. Research Synthesis (1.5 – 2 Pages)
Don’t just list your sources; synthesize them. Group your research into themes that matter to a CEO.
* **Industry Benchmarking:** What are competitors doing? 
* **Technical Viability:** Based on your research, what IT frameworks (following the **SDLC**) are proven to solve this specific problem?
* **Market Trends:** Why is this the right time for this solution?

### 2. The Recommendation (1 Page)
Describe the solution in plain English.
* **The "What":** A clear description of the IT fix/tool.
* **The "How":** High-level functionality. (Example: *"We will implement an automated inventory tracking system using RFID integration,"* rather than listing specific SKU numbers for scanners).
* **The Strategic Fit:** How this solution directly aligns with the company's **brand values** (e.g., if the company prides itself on "Customer First," explain how this IT fix reduces wait times).

### 3. Decision Criteria & Weighting (1 Page)
The professor explicitly asked for a **Decision Matrix Table**. This is your "Defensible Process."

| Criteria | Weight | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Operational Feasibility** | 30% | Must integrate with existing legacy systems without downtime. |
| **$ROI$ (3-Year)** | 25% | Project must reach a positive $NPV$ by the end of Year 3. |
| **Security/Compliance** | 25% | Must meet PCI-DSS or ADA standards. |
| **Scalability** | 20% | Must support a 15% annual growth in data volume. |

> **Note:** Use your data here. Why did this solution score higher than the "Credible Alternatives" you researched?

### 4. Business Justification & Financials (1 Page)
Translate the IT fix into "Executive Language."
* **The 3-Year Timeline:** * **Year 1:** Implementation costs and negative $ROI$.

### 5. Risk & Responsibility (1 Page)
This is where you demonstrate **Realistic Thinking.**

* **Legal/Ethical Impact:** Explicitly mention compliance (ADA, GDPR, etc.) and the ethical implications of the data you are touching.
